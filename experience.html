Technical diligence

Four physical checks before capital moves.

  1. Subsoil and bearing capacityBorehole samples and penetration tests show whether the site needs piling or shallow footings.
  2. Hydrology and flood exposureHigh flood level data and runoff paths confirm elevation and clear drainage.
  3. Underground and overhead encumbrancesRadar and wayleave registers reveal gas, storm, fibre and high-tension constraints.
  4. Cadastral demarcationDGPS traverses are laid over revenue maps to expose overlaps and possession gaps.
